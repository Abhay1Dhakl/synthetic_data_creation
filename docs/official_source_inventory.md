Use these as your first-pass collection sources for English seed text.
They are better starting points than random blogs because terminology is more stable and medically reviewed.

## Recommended Collection Order

1. Collect broad overview pages first.
2. Then collect symptom pages and diagnosis pages.
3. Then collect treatment, procedure, and aftercare pages.
4. Only after that add disease-specific technical review content.

## What To Avoid

- commercial hospital marketing pages
- anonymous health blogs
- pages with no review date or institutional owner
- pages dominated by legal, donation, or scheduling information

## Good Source Types For Parallel Data

- "What is X?"
- symptoms and warning signs
- risk factors
- diagnosis and tests
- treatment overview
- before-procedure instructions
- aftercare instructions
- when to call a doctor
- lifestyle and prevention guidance
